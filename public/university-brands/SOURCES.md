# University mark provenance

Official university web assets researched on 8 October 2026. The local files are used to identify KOC campus records. They do not imply university endorsement.

All 48 institution/campus entries have a mark. **Colleges** is a collective KOC group, so it deliberately uses a neutral group icon. UWE and Glenside share the UWE Bristol mark; UOL – Manchester uses the University of Law mark; Cheltenham uses the University of Gloucestershire mark; Medway uses the Universities at Medway mark.

Inline SVG marks were extracted from the university header or footer without recreating artwork. The Brunel combined header asset is displayed through a viewport that excludes the separate University of London partner mark. The City St George’s logo was taken from its official research repository; only surrounding whitespace was removed. Contrast backing follows the supplied white/dark mark variant, with no recolouring of university artwork.
